Multi-Entity Governance & Scope Selection
SECTION 01
GLOBAL CONTROLS
NAVIGATION: Universal Top Header ➔ Global 4-Tier Scope Selector

Acconeer ERP enforces a unified 4-tier scoping hierarchy positioned in the top global navigation bar. All transactional entry, ledger inquiries, and reporting outputs are dynamically governed by the active scope parameters selected by the user.

The 4-Tier Operational Scope Hierarchy

Hierarchy Level Selector Element Operational Instance Example Functional Scope Impact
Tier 1 Organization Anson Enterprises Global parent holding; defines shared COA templates, base currency, and security governance.
Tier 2 Legal Entity Anson Enterprises Legal Entity LLC Tax-registered operating corporate entity; holds legal TRN and dedicated general ledger accounts.
Tier 3 Branch Scope All Branches (Consolidated) • Dubai HQ Physical warehouse location, branch retail office, or cost-center for segmental profitability.
Tier 4 Fiscal Period FY 2026 • Q3 2026 • Monthly Active operational date filter governing transaction queries, dashboards, and report generation.

Operational Scoping Rules & Data Boundaries

Control Dimension System Architectural Rule Operational Impact on Screen
Session Filter Sync Dropdown changes update all active widgets instantly. Switching entities re-queries database; displays only data belonging to that entity.
Branch Cost Tagging Every invoice, bill, and voucher tags the active branch. Enables branch-level P&L and inventory tracking without separate legal companies.
Cross-Entity Isolation Strict sub-ledger isolation between operating units. Invoices created in Entity A never alter balances in Entity B without an inter-company voucher.

Fiscal Period Lifecycle States & Posting Permissions

Period State Status Pill Display Permitted Operational Transactions in Acconeer ERP
Open Operational OPEN (Green Pill) Unrestricted transactional entry across billing, supplier bills, receipts, and inventory.
Soft Monthly Close SOFT_CLOSE (Amber Pill) Commercial sub-ledgers locked; only authorized accountants may post adjusting journals.
Hard Fiscal Lock LOCKED (Red Pill) Permanent freeze; all ledger modifications rejected; requires CFO override to reopen.

Multi-Currency & Inter-Company Elimination Framework

Governance Dimension System Architectural Rule Accounting & Compliance Behavior
Base Functional Currency Standardized to AED for UAE statutory compliance. All financial statements and VAT declarations calculate and report in Dirhams.
Foreign Currency Support Supports USD, EUR, GBP, SAR line item pricing. Converts to AED via daily spot rates; automatically calculates realized FX gain/loss.
Inter-Company Accounts Reciprocal balancing entries between affiliate entities. Posts balancing vouchers to Due from Affiliates (1950) and Due to Affiliates (2950).
Consolidated Elimination Dynamic netting when selecting All Entities. Eliminates internal reciprocal balances to present true external enterprise performance.
Entity Tax & Fiscal Bounds Registered 15-digit TRN & 12-month accounting cycle. Enforces period lock cutoffs, statutory tax invoice generation, and fiscal closure.
CROSS-ENTITY POSTING PROTECTION
Each legal entity operates with an isolated sub-ledger. Invoices and bills created under Anson Enterprises Legal Entity LLC will not alter receivables or payables of sibling entities. Cross-entity trading automatically generates balancing inter-company vouchers.
