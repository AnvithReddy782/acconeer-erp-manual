Operational Scoping Rules & Data Boundaries

Session Filtering vs Period Locking

The top-bar period selector is an active view filter for your current session. Selecting Q3 2026 displays transactions posted within that date window. Period locking is an administrative control executed in General Ledger settings to prevent postings to closed months.

Branch Cost-Center Tagging

Every sales invoice, purchase bill, and inventory intake document captures the active Branch ID. This enables granular branch-level trial balances and warehouse inventory segregation without requiring separate legal entities.

Standard Operating Procedure: Setting Your Active Work Scope

  1. Sign in to the Acconeer ERP portal (acconeer.zestwings.com) using your corporate credentials.
  2. Inspect the top-left navigation bar to verify your assigned Organization name.
  3. Click the Legal Entity dropdown and select the operating company you are transacting for (e.g. Gulf Horizon General Trading LLC). For consolidated group reports, select All Entities.
  4. Select the target Branch Scope (e.g. Dubai Head Office or Warehouse 01).
  5. Choose the active Fiscal Reporting Period (e.g. Q3 2026) to ensure your workspace aligns with the current transaction cycle.
  6. Verify that the status pill displays OPEN. If the period displays CLOSED, new transaction postings are disabled.

Reconciliation Interface Fields & Balancing Calculations

Interface Field Description & Operating Rule System Validation & Balancing Formula
Statement Ending Balance The exact closing balance displayed on the official bank statement. Manually entered by user; must match statement PDF/feed.
Book Balance The current closing balance of the linked GL bank account. Calculated dynamically: Class 1010 Account Net Balance.
Cleared Balance Sum of all checked and matched debits less credits. Calculated: Opening Cleared + Matched Receipts - Matched Payments.
Unreconciled Difference Variance between Statement Ending Balance and Cleared Balance. Formula: Statement Ending Balance - Cleared Balance = 0.00 AED.

Standard Operating Procedure: Executing Bank Reconciliation

  1. Select the corporate Bank Account from the top dropdown (e.g. Emirates NBD - AED Operating).
  2. Enter the official Statement Ending Date and Statement Ending Balance from your bank statement.
  3. Click Import Statement to upload an electronic bank statement file (OFX, QIF, or standardized CSV).
  4. Click the green Auto-Match button. The system scans entries and automatically pairs identical amounts and reference dates.
  5. For remaining unmatched items, manually check the corresponding checkboxes in the Statement and Ledger panels.
  6. Verify that the Unreconciled Difference displays exactly 0.00 AED.
  7. Click Finalize Reconciliation to lock the statement period and mark entries as permanently cleared.

The Automated 3-Way Match Verification Protocol

Comparison Pillar Source Document Reference System Verification & Tolerance Rule
Purchase Order (PO) Authorized procurement order and agreed unit prices. Billed unit price must match approved PO line price exactly (0.00% variance).
Goods Received Note Physical warehouse inspection count and accepted quantity. Billed quantity cannot exceed total accepted items on linked GRN.

Standard Operating Procedure: Logging and Approving a Vendor Bill

  1. From the Bills workspace (Alt + P), click the blue + Enter Bill button.
  2. Select the Vendor Name and choose the corresponding Purchase Order Number from the lookup list.
  3. Enter the vendor's official invoice number in the Supplier Bill Reference field and verify the invoice date.
  4. Link the warehouse GRN Intake Number to pull the verified received quantities automatically.
  5. Inspect the system 3-Way Match Indicator. A green match checkmark confirms quantity and price agreement.
  6. Verify the calculated 5% Input VAT amount against the physical invoice.
  7. Click Submit for Approval. Once approved by the Finance Manager, the bill posts to Accounts Payable (Account 2100).

Discrepancy Tiers & Commercial Dispute Resolution

Exception Type System Match Status Resolution Procedure
Quantity Shortage GRN_QTY < BILLED_QTY Reduce billed line quantity to match GRN; vendor must bill remainder later.

VARIANCE EXCEPTION HANDLING
If a price or quantity discrepancy exceeds corporate tolerance, the system flags the bill with MATCH EXCEPTION. The bill cannot be posted for payment until the vendor issues an adjusting credit note or the buyer re-authorizes the price increase.
